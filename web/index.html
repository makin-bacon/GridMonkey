4 Text styles

Body, three heading levels, and bullet and numbered lists two levels deep. Every line lands on the baseline grid, and every indent is a whole grid unit.

Body —

The weight is used for Body, Body First and every list style. Body First and the lists also take Body's grid alignment. After lists adds space after the last item of a bullet or numbered list; items in a list stay tight.

Headings —

½ line needs a ÷2 or ÷4 baseline grid. On those grids a heading gets extra space after if needed, so the next body line still lands on a full baseline. First line only = InDesign's Align to Grid: First Line Only.
